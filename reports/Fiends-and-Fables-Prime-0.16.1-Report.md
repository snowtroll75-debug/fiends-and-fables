# Fiends & Fables Prime 0.16.1

Requires TRG Prime 3.637.1.

- **Flame Beetle, Fire Mote:** its item attack bonus is now 0, not −2. Live testing on 3.637.1 showed core gives a natural missile "BAB and ability +1" and "Ranged (Dexterity) +2". With the −2 it rolled +1; it now rolls the printed +3. Damage stays 1d6 fire with no Strength, and it needs no ammunition.
