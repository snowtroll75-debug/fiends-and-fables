# Fiends & Fables Prime 0.17.0

Fiends & Fables 3.0 block 8, Kothorn to Lynxara. Graham's rulings of 13 October 2026: "go with your recommendations". Requires TRG Prime 3.638.0.

- **R1 Kothorn:** the bear's kit is replaced with its own.
  - **Removed:** Bite, Claw, Monstrous Grab, Sudden Charge and Skill Focus (Swim).
  - **Added:** Gore (+11, 1d8+6 piercing), Powerful Charge, Acute Sense (Smell) and Great Fortitude.
  - **Senses:** Nightvision 120 ft and Scent 70 ft.
  - **Fortitude:** stays at +18. Great Fortitude now supplies the +2 that a hidden adjustment carried.
- **R2 Bulette:** the Behir's kit is replaced with its own.
  - **Removed:** Cleave, Power Attack, Monstrous Grab, Constrict, Swallow, Electricity Breath, Storm of Claws, Trackless Step, Wise Skills and Skill Focus (Awareness). The Magical trait comes off its Bite and Claw.
  - **Added:** Acute Sense (Smell), Great Fortitude, Iron Will, Natural Leaper, Physical Training, Pouncing Assault and Toughness.
  - **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Nightvision 120 ft and Scent 70 ft.
  - **Printed figures kept:** Fort +24, Will +11, HP 188 and Athletics +19. Its feats now supply what hidden adjustments carried.
- **R3 Bugbear:** loses the Soldier's Iron Will and Power Attack. Will stays at +2.
- **R4 Bugbear weapons:** stale adjustments removed. The Javelins now roll +5 for 1d6+2 and +8 for 1d6+4. The Greatswords roll 2d6+6 and 2d6+8, as printed.
- **R5 Cheetah's Sprint:** now automated on the compound-action rail.
  - It costs 3 AP and moves up to 10 × Speed.
  - It allows one Bite or one Claw at +4 circumstance (the Charge bonus); core 3.638.0's `choose: 1` refuses the second.
  - It can be used once an hour.
- **R6 Lynxara:** the printed +25 is read as +5. Bite and Claw now roll +5.
- **R7 Smilodon:** its Bite gains AR 2.

The tools are `module-block8.mjs` and `verify-module-block8.mjs`. Blocks 3 to 8 all verify green.
