# Fiends & Fables Prime 0.10.8 — Change Log

## Added

- Vrock worked creature Actor and Vrock Demon Heritage.
- Reusable Lycanthrope Race for worked creature examples.
- Werebat worked creature Actor and Heritage.
- Werebear worked creature Actor and Heritage.
- Wereboar worked creature Actor and Heritage.
- Werecat worked creature Actor and Heritage.
- Common lycanthrope rules cards: Animal Empathy, Luna Frenzy, Lycanthropic Curse and Shift Form.
- Vrock Cloud of Spores, Dance of Ruin and Stunning Screech.
- Vrock Divinity innate Dimension Door and Mirror Image as Focus spells.
- Werecat standard starting ammunition: 20 arrows.

## Corrected implementation gap

Earlier releases treated the existence of Werebat/Werebear/Wereboar/Werecat transformation Templates as a reason to skip their complete printed worked stat blocks. The source inventory distinguishes reusable Templates from complete creature stat blocks. Version 0.10.8 corrects that gap by adding the ready-made Actors while retaining the Templates unchanged.

## Source fidelity

- Uses author-approved purchased Ability allocations.
- Keeps Type/Race/Heritage/size effects separate from purchased scores.
- Retains Werecat's printed Stealthy Strike (2d6).
- Splits Werebear Handaxe into melee and thrown records so both printed attack configurations can be represented.
- Uses the detailed Wereboar Level 4 / EL 3 stat block and approved allocation ledger where the summary table conflicts.
- Does not invent missing Cat's Landing mechanics.
- Preserves all descriptions as plain text.

## Retained

- All 285 previous creature Actor roots byte-identical.
- All 13 reusable transformation Templates unchanged.
- Complete eight-species true-dragon framework unchanged.
- All true-dragon Breath Weapons remain Focus and 0 PP.
- Creature compendium Type filter unchanged.
- TRG Prime core system files modified: none.
